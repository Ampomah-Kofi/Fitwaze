If you feel shaky, sweaty, dizzy or confused, your blood sugar may be low.

  1. Take 15 g of fast sugar: half a glass of juice or 3–4 glucose tablets.
  2. Rest for 15 minutes, then check your blood sugar if you can.
  3. Don't keep walking until you feel better.
